Swara is a lightweight, native Android music player built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose (Material Design 3), and AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer).

Features:
- Background playback with lock-screen, Bluetooth/smartwatch, and Android Auto support
- Floating Dynamic Island miniplayer & 8 customizable Home Screen widgets
- 5-band Equalizer, Bass Boost, Room Reverb, Sound Styles, and Crossfade
- Synced lyrics with optional Romaji/Hangul romanization
- Offline downloads, local FLAC/MP3/WAV playback, and playlist importer
- Zero ads, zero trackers, 100% open source under GPL-3.0
